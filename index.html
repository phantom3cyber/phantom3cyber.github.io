About the Founder

The expertise and vision behind Phantom3 Cyber.

Dr. Arun Viswanathan

Dr. Viswanathan is a seasoned cybersecurity expert with over two decades of experience in research and engineering. His work focuses on AI/ML-driven security solutions, cyber risk assessments, penetration testing, and large-scale data analysis.

With experience spanning startups, Microsoft, JPL, and academia, he tackles hard problems and delivers practical, high-impact security results across aerospace and critical infrastructure.

He holds a PhD in Computer Science from USC, is well-published with 450+ citations, and holds a U.S. patent for a novel cyber risk assessment tool.
