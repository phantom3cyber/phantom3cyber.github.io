About the Founder

Dr. Arun Viswanathan

Dr. Viswanathan is a seasoned cybersecurity expert with over two decades of experience in research and engineering. His work focuses on AI/ML-driven security solutions, cyber risk assessments, penetration testing, and large-scale data analysis. He has a proven track record of developing innovative security tools and leading high-impact research teams.

With extensive experience spanning startups, large organizations like Microsoft and JPL, and academia, Dr. Viswanathan thrives on tackling hard, ill-defined problems. His background in both theoretical research and practical implementation has led to significant contributions in aerospace and critical infrastructure security. He is a recognized leader in the aerospace cybersecurity community, holding leadership roles in AIAA and IEEE.

He holds a PhD in Computer Science from the University of Southern California, is a well-published researcher with hundreds of citations, and holds a U.S. patent for a novel cyber risk assessment tool.
